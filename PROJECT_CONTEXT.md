## 2. Project idea (chosen)
**Rental Agreement Risk Analyzer (Pune):** reads a Leave & License agreement, splits it into clauses, extracts facts (rent, deposit, %, days, area, BHK), classifies each clause and rates risk **Low/Medium/High** with a reason, giving an overall risk score and "NOT STATED" warnings.
**Design = hybrid:** Pune-calibrated **rules for numeric clauses** + **ML (TF-IDF + linear/NB models) for wording-based clauses** + a transparent **red-flag phrase lexicon**.

## 4. Official format (Government of Maharashtra, IGR) — `reference/Maharashtra_IGR_Leave_and_License_format.pdf`
Source: https://efilingigr.maharashtra.gov.in/ereg/downloadstuff/LAndL.pdf (registration is mandatory under Sec. 55 Maharashtra Rent Control Act 1999 — cited from a legal blog; verify before the report).
11 clauses: 1 Period, 2 Rent & Deposit (6 alternatives: fixed/staged rent x refundable/non-refundable deposit; says "compensation" not "rent"), 3 Payment of deposit, 4 Maintenance (Licensor pays all OR Licensee pays maintenance), 5 Use, 6 Alteration, 7 No tenancy/sublet, 8 Inspection, 9 Cancellation (mutual 1-month notice), 10 Possession (double daily damages; removal without recourse to Court), 11 Registration (costs on Licensee) + Schedule + signature block. Contains PII fields (name, PAN, UID, address).
**Gaps in the official form** (used for "NOT STATED" check): no lock-in, yearly increase, late fee, deposit-refund timeline, utilities terms. **No BHK or area field** next to rent -> the app takes area & BHK as inputs.
